Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

"GTA VI Top Moments uploader" is a private tool run by the owner of the GTA VI Top Moments YouTube channel. It is used only to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It has a single user, the channel owner. Nobody else can sign in to it.

YouTube

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to upload videos, set their title and description, post a comment crediting the original streamer on our own videos, and read our own channel's statistics. It doesn't read, collect or store data about any other user, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

The login token is kept as an encrypted secret. You can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
